1. Project Overview & Business Model

The ELAKIYAA Client Management System is an enterprise workforce allocation and shift management platform built for ELAKIYAA, an official Manpower Supply Agency.

💡 Real-World Business Model:
• ELAKIYAA (The Agency): Recruits, manages, and supplies trained field attendants, security staff, and cashiers.
• Clients (Petrol Bunks & Enterprises): Indian Oil (IOCL), Bharat Petroleum (BPCL), Hindustan Petroleum (HPCL), Shell, and Nayara Energy petrol bunks that hire workforce from ELAKIYAA.
• Staff (The Workforce): Employees deployed at client petrol stations who perform daily shifts and record attendance.
• Managers: Area supervisors from ELAKIYAA who oversee deployed staff and coordinate daily shift rosters.

5. Database Architecture & Data Models

Located in supabase/schema.sql, the relational cloud database is structured into 4 primary core tables:

Table Name Primary Fields Operational Purpose
users id, name, mobile, role, photo, status Stores all 23 user accounts across Admin, Manager, Staff, and Client roles.
attendance user_id, date, punch_in, status, photo_url, coordinates Records daily biometric punch-ins, camera image proof, GPS coordinates, and late minutes.
tasks title, staff_ids, client_ids, due_date, priority, status Tracks multi-staff & multi-bunk task allocations, deadlines, and completion states.
audit_logs user_name, action, module, description, ip_address, timestamp Provides immutable security logging of all administrative and attendance edits.
