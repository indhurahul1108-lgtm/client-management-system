🌟 ELAKIYAA — Client & Workforce Management System

Live on Vercel React 19 Vite 8 Tailwind CSS 3.4

📌 1. Executive Summary

ELAKIYAA Client & Workforce Management System is a production-ready enterprise solution built for manpower staffing, client service tracking, and distributed shift management. Customized for oil & gas retail networks (Indian Oil, Bharat Petroleum, Hindustan Petroleum, Shell, Nayara) and enterprise service providers, it unifies Admin governance, Manager supervision, Staff operational reporting, and Client transparency into a seamless, high-performance web platform.

• 🌐 Live Application Link: https://client-management-system-iota.vercel.app
• 💻 GitHub Source Code: https://github.com/indhurahul1108-lgtm/client-management-system

🏢 2. Business Problem & Solution

Traditional Industry Challenges ELAKIYAA Enterprise Solution
Buddy Punching & False Attendance Biometric Camera & Geo-Stamped Attendance — Real-time front-camera capture with embedded GPS coordinates, address, and tamper-proof timestamp overlay on HTML5 canvas.
Disconnected Task Allocations Multi-Entity Task Engine — Bulk task assignments across multiple staff members and multiple client stations simultaneously.
Lack of Client Visibility Dedicated Client Portal — Real-time tracking of assigned staff, shift logs, official document downloads, and service request tracking.

👥 3. Role-Based Portals & Key Features

👑 1. Admin Portal (/admin)

  • Executive Dashboard: Workforce KPIs, attendance ratios, and bunk counts.
  • User Governance: Search, filter, activate/deactivate accounts.
  • Staff & Manager Directory: Direct telephonic calling and branch assignments.
  • Multi-Entity Tasks: Assign tasks across multiple staff and bunks simultaneously.
  • Attendance Control: Daily punch-in verification with audit-logged edit modal.
  • Salary & Payroll: Monthly net salary register and payment tracking.
  • Audit Logging: Immutable security log of all system changes.

👔 2. Manager Portal (/manager)

  • Supervisory Dashboard: Active team presence and pending approvals.
  • Staff Roster: Inspection of staff assigned to manager with workload.
  • Bunk Inspection: Station coverage, satisfaction, and shift monitoring.
  • Leave Authorization: Approve/reject staff leaves and apply personal leave.
  • Work Allocation: Assign tasks directly to team members with progress bars.

👷 3. Staff Portal (/staff)

  • Camera Punch In/Out: Live camera capture with GPS stamp and 10:10 AM grace policy.
  • Task Execution: Real-time progress slider (0% to 100%) and status updates.
  • Leave Requests: Apply for sick, casual, or annual leave with manager alerts.
  • Digital Payslips: View monthly salary breakdowns and deduction slips.
  • Documents Locker: Access company letters, policies, and ID proofs.

🏢 4. Client Portal (/client)

  • Station Overview: Real-time view of deployed workforce on site.
  • Duty Confirmation: 1-click confirmation acknowledging on-duty staff.
  • Direct Calling: One-touch phone calling to assigned staff & supervisor.
  • Service Requests: Raise tickets for extra staff, uniform, or equipment.
  • Document Repository: Instant download of legal SLAs, invoices, and certificates.
